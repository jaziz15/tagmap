# TagMap

TagMap is a Chrome extension for analysts and product managers. It shows the **Segment**
analytics events (`track`, `page`, `identify`) a website fires, live in a side panel, and
lets you save them as named funnels to refer back to while you build your analysis.

- **Segment only, for now.** Works on sites using Segment's analytics.js or analytics-next.
  Other analytics tools are not detected yet.
- **Runs only where you say so.** TagMap has no access to any website until you enable it
  for that site, and you can switch it off again at any time.
- **Observed events stay in your browser.** Only funnels you choose to save are stored in
  your account, and only if you sign in with Google.
